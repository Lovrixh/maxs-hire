What Size Generator Do I Need? NZ Sizing Guide

The most common question we get, and the honest answer is: it depends on what you're running, and how much extra it pulls when it switches on. Here's how to get it right.

1. Add up your running watts

Every appliance has a running wattage, the power it uses once it's going. List everything you'll run at once and add them up. Here are typical figures:

ApplianceRunning wattsStart-up surge
LED / string lighting~300 WNone
Phone & EFTPOS~150 WNone
Fridge / chiller~350 W2 to 3x
Chest freezer~400 W2 to 3x
Coffee machine~1,600 WLow
Bain-marie / food warmer~1,500 WNone
Deep fryer (electric)~2,400 WNone
Power tools~1,400 W2x
Air compressor~1,800 W3x
Water pump~1,200 W3x

PA / sound system~800 WLow
Patio / space heater~2,000 WNone

2. Allow for start-up surge, the bit people miss

Anything with a motor or compressor (fridges, freezers, pumps, compressors) briefly pulls 2–3× its running watts when it starts. Size for the running total plus the biggest single start-up surge, or it'll trip out.

3. Add headroom

Never run flat out. Add ~20% headroom so the generator runs cool and copes with surprises. Our size calculator does all three steps automatically.

4. kVA vs kW

kVA is total power available; kW is what your gear actually uses. Rough rule: kW ≈ kVA × 0.8.

Typical picks from our fleet

  • Honda EU22i (2.2 kVA), stall, coffee cart, small wedding, lights + EFTPOS.
  • Honda EU32i (3.2 kVA), busier stall or small food truck.
  • Honda EU70is (7 kVA), full food truck, trades, larger events.
  • Denyo 60 kVA diesel, construction, three-phase, standby, big events.

FAQs

What size generator for a coffee cart?

A single espresso machine needs ~1500–2000 W running but surges higher on start-up. A EU22i (2.2 kVA) suits a simple cart; add a grinder, fridge and lights and step up to the EU32i (3.2 kVA).

What size generator for a wedding?

Depends on catering and lighting. A quiet inverter suits many marquees; larger receptions with electric catering may want the 60 kVA diesel. See our wedding page or use the calculator.
