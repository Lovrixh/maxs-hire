Generator Hire for a Power Outage NZ

Storms, network faults and planned outages all cut power when you least want it. A hire generator bridges the gap, here's how to do it safely and get the essentials back on fast.

First: what do you actually need to power?

You rarely need to run the whole house. Prioritise the essentials: fridge and freezer (to save your food), lighting, wifi/router, phone and device charging, and heating if needed. For a shop: tills, EFTPOS, fridges and lights.

What size?

For home essentials a 2.2–3.2 kVA inverter is often enough; add heating or a big freezer and the 7 kVA EU70is is safer. Businesses with more load may need the 60 kVA diesel. Use the calculator or call us.

Safety first, always

  • Run it outdoors only, in a well-ventilated spot, never inside a house, garage or enclosed space (exhaust fumes are deadly).
  • Keep it dry and on stable ground; use an RCD-protected distribution and correctly rated leads.
  • Don't overload it, size with headroom.

FAQs

Can I run my whole house on a hire generator?

Usually you don't need to, powering essentials (fridge, lights, wifi, heating) is cheaper and simpler. Whole-home backup needs a larger unit and proper changeover; ask us and we'll advise.
