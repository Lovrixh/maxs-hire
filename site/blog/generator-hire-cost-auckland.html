How Much Does Generator Hire Cost in Auckland?

"How much is it to hire a generator?" is a fair first question, but the honest answer is that it depends on a few things. Here's what shapes the price so you can budget with confidence.

The four things that set your price

1. The unit. A small 2.2 kVA inverter is far cheaper than a 60 kVA three-phase diesel. Match the machine to the job (our calculator helps) so you're not paying for power you won't use.

2. Duration. Day, weekend, week or long-term, the longer the hire, the better the effective rate. Long-term site hires are structured differently to a one-day event.

3. Delivery & collection. Quoted by location across Auckland. Being local keeps this competitive, and you'll see it upfront.

4. Dry vs operated hire. Dry hire is equipment only; operated hire adds an operator and fuel management for events, film and standby.

What we won't do

No hidden fees. Delivery, fuel and pickup are all in your quote before you commit. If a smaller, cheaper unit will do the job, we'll tell you.

Get an accurate number

Because every job differs, we quote each one properly. Tell us the unit (or let us recommend one), your dates, location and what you're powering, and we'll come back fast.

FAQs

Is it cheaper to hire or buy a generator?

For occasional or one-off needs, hiring is almost always cheaper: no purchase, servicing, storage or compliance costs, and you get the right size for each job. Buying only makes sense for constant, long-term use.

Do longer hires cost less per day?

Yes. Weekly, monthly and long-term hires have better effective rates than a single day. Tell us your timeframe and we'll structure it.
