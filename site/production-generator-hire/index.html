FAQs

How quiet are your generators for filming?

Our Honda inverters run around 48 to 57 dB(A), quieter than a normal conversation, and the 60 kVA diesel is in an ultra-silent canopy. With sensible placement and longer cable runs, the generator stays off your sound recording.

Can you be on set before call time?

Yes. We deliver and get you running before call, and collect after wrap, including early starts, night shoots, weekends and multi-day jobs.

Do you supply distro and cabling?

Yes. Lifeguard 32A and 63A RCD distribution boards, 3-phase feeders and 10A to 63A leads, all tested and tagged, can be added to any generator hire.

Can you invoice our production company or agency?

Yes. Send the job details and a PO number if you use them, and we'll quote and invoice the production company or agency directly.

Can you power a full unit base?

Yes. The 60 kVA diesel covers most unit bases, and we can split power to catering, makeup and wardrobe with distro. For bigger shoots we arrange 120 to 300 kVA sets through our network.

Can you help with short-notice shoots and reshoots?

Often, yes. Call 021 0877 1003 with the date, location and load and we'll tell you straight away what we can do.
